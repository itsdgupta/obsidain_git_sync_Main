Here is a curated color palette designed specifically for a **premium, warm, and minimal** coffee brand:

---

### Core Palette

| Role                         | Color Name      | Hex Code  | Purpose & Application                                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Base / Canvas**            | Warm Alabaster  | `#F9F6F0` | Main site background; keeps the design minimal and editorial without the harshness of pure white. |
| **Primary Text / Structure** | Espresso Roast  | `#1E1714` | Body text, headings, footers, thin divider lines. Deep and rich rather than sterile black.        |
| **Secondary Neutral**        | Oat Milk        | `#E8DFC8` | Card backgrounds, section blocks, image borders, secondary containers.                            |
| **Primary Accent**           | Burnished Amber | `#C68B59` | Primary CTAs ("Order Now", "Explore Roasts"), link hovers, active navigation states.              |
| **Deep Supporting Tone**     | Smoked Chestnut | `#4A3326` | Secondary buttons, badge borders, drop caps, sub-headers.                                         |
| **Muted Text / Meta**        | Stone Taupe     | `#8C827A` | Captions, timestamps, disabled states, product metadata (origin, roast level).                    |

---

### Hierarchy & Usage (60-30-10 Rule)

* **60% Dominant Base (`#F9F6F0` & `#E8DFC8`):** Use generous whitespace. The generous breathing room around photography and text gives the site its *minimal* and modern feel.
* **30% Contrast & Structure (`#1E1714` & `#4A3326`):** Delivers clean typography, structural balance, and the rich, organic depth of roasted coffee.
* **10% Accent Pop (`#C68B59`):** Reserve for critical user actions so they stand out effortlessly without competing with your product photography.

---

### Recommended Typography Pairings

To complement this palette and reinforce the premium, warm aesthetic:

* **Headings:** High-contrast serif with warmth (e.g., *Ogg*, *Playfair Display*, or *Canela*).
* **Body & UI:** Clean, humanistic sans-serif (e.g., *Satoshi*, *Inter*, or *General Sans*).
